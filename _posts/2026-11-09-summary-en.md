### [KetchUp brings an open-source SketchUp alternative to the browser with Rust and WebGPU](https://ketchup3d.github.io/app/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

KetchUp is an open-source, browser-based 3D modeler built in Rust with WebGPU and presented as an alternative to SketchUp. It drew positive Hacker News discussion for its cross-platform web interface, with commenters describing it as a spiritual successor to SketchUp. The supplied item provides no version, license, file-format compatibility, or measured performance details, so those capabilities remain unconfirmed.

hackernews · dcoulmbe · Nov 8, 22:35 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50418176)

**「Background」** SketchUp established a face-based modeling workflow widely used for architecture, woodworking, and furniture design; KetchUp&\#x27;s project page targets the same cabinetry and furniture use case. Its repository describes the app as a free, open-source SketchUp alternative built with Rust, WebAssembly, and WebGPU that runs in a browser or on desktop.

**「Impact」** Because KetchUp runs in the browser on WebGPU, users can try it without installing a desktop application, but the supplied material does not state which browsers or GPUs are supported.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters praised the Rust-based browser UI and text rendering, and one asked for feature showcases on the landing page. Another reported that drawing a rectangle prompts for an object name and said that default behavior seems wrong.

### [Goldman Sachs reportedly pulls Claude Code from bankers and traders](https://www.thestateofai.com/news/goldman-sachs-claude-code-traders-bankers) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Goldman Sachs is reportedly pulling Anthropic&\#x27;s Claude Code from its bankers and traders, according to a report in The State of AI. The supplied item does not say how many employees are affected, when the change takes effect, or Goldman&\#x27;s stated reason. It also provides no confirmation from Goldman or Anthropic, so the scope and rationale remain unverified.

hackernews · TheWiggles · Nov 8, 18:16 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50415628)

**「Background」** Claude Code is Anthropic&\#x27;s agentic coding tool, and Goldman had made it available beyond its engineering staff; according to the report, bankers and traders used it to track client flows and build market models. A commenter citing the article said the reported restriction applies only to bankers and traders in Hong Kong, not to the firm&\#x27;s engineers.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters offered competing explanations for the reported pullback: one argued that semi-unattended coding agents on finance workstations create an unacceptable security exposure because those machines also reach client and trade data, email, the internet, and internal networks, while another said their organization had cut Claude access because of chronic billing overages and Anthropic&\#x27;s lack of a normalized rate. Others pointed to bank compliance and audit requirements, and one commenter questioned the timing relative to Goldman&\#x27;s role leading Anthropic&\#x27;s IPO.
